Usage Rules

🔒

Gold is the athlete side. Blue is the coach side. They never mix in one ad.

The One Rule

01 · System

One mark, two roles: full lockup and the numeral alone.

The "2" carries the brand on its own in tight spaces — app icons, social avatars, favicons. The full lockup is for anywhere there's room to say the name.

Do

  • Use the gold mark on black, white, or blue backgrounds
  • Drop to the numeral-only mark below 200px wide — the full lockup stops being legible
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the "2" on all sides
  • Pair gold accents with athlete-side content, blue with coach-side — never both in one lockup

Don't

  • Recolor the mark outside gold, black, white, or blue
  • Add a background shape, glow, or shadow behind it
  • Imply an existing track record with the mark — no "trusted by" badges until there's a real one

02 · System

Near-black, gold, and blue. Nothing else carries meaning.

Near-black is the constant stage. Gold means athlete. Blue means coach. Green is reserved for the platform's own success states — never used in ad creative.

03 · System

One committed display face. Everything else stays quiet.

Twenty Five Select's type system is younger than Quarter Athletic's — Impact is the one display face locked in so far, reserved for stat numbers and short, punchy badges. Body copy runs on a clean system sans, deliberately unremarkable so the real UI screenshots stay the visual hero.

04 · System

No black box. Say the mechanism, not just the promise.

Every headline names the actual thing that makes it true — a human reviewer, a real factor score, a real screenshot. Never a vague trust claim standing alone.

05 · System

Real photography. Real screenshots. Never a fabricated UI.

Unlike Quarter Athletic's ghost-mannequin renders, every product proof point here is a genuine screenshot — Verification, Match Factors, Match Preview — composited into editorial photography of real settings. An AI render may never attempt UI, a logo, or a crest.

TrustNear-black, high contrast
Athlete sideWarm gold rim light
Coach sideCool blue ambient
ProductGreen reserved for in-app only

Do

  • Reserve a clean empty panel in every prompt for the real screenshot to composite in afterward
  • Use editorial sports-lifestyle or sports-recruiting photography style, never stock-cliché smiling-at-camera
  • Keep gold lighting for athlete-side scenes, cool blue for coach-side

Don't

  • Let an AI render attempt any screen content, UI, or logo
  • Mix athlete-gold and coach-blue lighting in the same frame outside the two unified months
  • Show a stat or number the product can't actually back up yet
