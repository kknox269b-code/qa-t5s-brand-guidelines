The Roadmap

01 · System

The mark carries the truth. Never redraw it.

The wordmark and the QA circle are the only two approved forms. Both come from the same geometric structure — sharp terminals, no rounded joins — so the mark reads as engineered, not decorated.

Do

  • Use the gold or white lockup on navy, black, or photographic backgrounds
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the "Q" on all sides
  • Use the circle mark alone for small spaces — social avatars, favicons, jersey tags
  • Composite the mark in post from the real file — never let an AI render attempt it

Don't

  • Recolor the mark outside the five approved fills (gold, white, black, blue, red)
  • Stretch, skew, rotate, or add a drop shadow / bevel
  • Place gold on gold, or navy on black — protect the contrast
  • Pair the full wordmark with the circle mark in the same lockup

02 · System

One accent per month, one register per accent.

Rugby's navy never disappears — it's the constant underneath. Each month layers in exactly one more color, tied to a single emotional register, never mixed with another month's accent in the same asset.

03 · System

Ghost-mannequin, always. Never real product photography.

Every product shot is a photorealistic 3D render of a translucent grey mannequin — never a photograph of real apparel, and never a live athlete wearing product. One production system, reskinned by sport and lit to match that month's register.

Month 1Dawn/dusk, still
Month 2Golden hour, dynamic
Month 3Bright midday, real texture
Month 4Matched multi-era light
Month 5Wide, architectural

Do

  • Reserve blank space on every mannequin's chest for the logo — composited in post
  • Keep one focal point per frame; leave the reserved band empty for headline/tagline
  • Reuse a sport's lighting recipe when it reskins into a new sport

Don't

  • Let an AI render attempt the logo, crest, or any real screenshot
  • Photograph real jerseys or real athletes for product hero shots
  • Mix two months' lighting moods in one composite
